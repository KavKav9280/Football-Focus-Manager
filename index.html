Task Management System

Give a task a target date/time and — optionally — a countdown timer in minutes, plus a target goal to track progress toward. Turn on notifications below to get a real system alert when a timer starts, at the halfway point, with 5 minutes left, and when time's up (with a sound alarm). Priority sections sort themselves automatically: any section with active tasks stays on top, empty or fully-completed sections sink to the bottom — this can't be manually reordered. You can still reorder individual tasks within their own section using the arrows.

Calendar

Every task with a scheduled start time appears here as a block spanning its duration. The current block highlights live while it's active, with a countdown to the end. Log progress toward a task's target goal right on its block — it auto-completes the moment logged progress meets the goal.

Daily Habits
